Driver vs car / H3 and H4, tested once on the held-out 2025 season

Driver or car?

Two questions. Can corner-by-corner telemetry tell two teammates apart, in the same car, on weekends the model never saw? And when a driver moves to another team, does their style come with them?

H3 telling teammates apart

Which teammate drove this lap?

Balanced accuracy: how often the right teammate is named for a lap, averaged over the two drivers; 0.5 is guessing. For each team, a model is trained on all weekends but one and tested on the weekend left out, then the next weekend is left out, and so on (leave-one-weekend-out).

H4 drivers who changed teams

Does the style follow the driver?

Each driver's 2025 season signature (30 corner measurements, each relative to the field) is compared with their own 2024 signature, in another car for those who moved, and with their 2025 teammate's, in the same car on the same weekends. Above the diagonal, their own past is closer: the style followed the driver. Below it, the teammate is closer: the car, or adapting to it, dominated.

With five team changers, all five had to land above the line for a significant result (stated before the test). Colapinto also changed teams but was missing from the preregistered list; he is shown hollow and is not part of the test.
